01 The Challenge

Chlorine Demands the Highest Level of Chemical Protection

A major power plant in Kuwait required a comprehensive coating solution for pipelines carrying chlorine — one of the most aggressive chemicals encountered in power generation and water treatment facilities. Chlorine attacks carbon steel rapidly, causing accelerated pitting, general corrosion, and ultimately catastrophic pipeline failure if left unprotected.

The pipelines required both internal and external protection. Internally, the pipe surfaces are exposed to continuous chlorine contact, demanding a coating system with exceptional chemical resistance and zero permeability. Externally, the power plant environment — with humidity, heat, and chemical splash exposure — requires a durable, long-life protective barrier.

The client needed a turnkey solution that could be applied to flanged pipe spools, cured, inspected, and returned for installation within a tight maintenance window to minimize plant downtime.

Why Chlorine Is So Dangerous to Pipelines

Chlorine — whether in gas, liquid, or dissolved form — is one of the most corrosive substances handled in industrial facilities. It attacks carbon steel at rates exceeding 1.0 mm/year in wet conditions. When combined with moisture, chlorine forms hydrochloric acid (HCl) at the metal surface, creating an acid corrosion loop that accelerates metal loss exponentially. Standard paint systems fail within months under chlorine service. Only specialized high-performance linings such as glass flake vinyl ester can provide reliable long-term protection.

02 Our Solution

Dual Coating System — Internal Glass Flake Vinyl Ester & External Protective Coating

Primary Surface Solutions engineered a dual-system approach to protect the chlorine pipelines from the inside out. The internal surfaces received a glass flake vinyl ester lining applied using a centrifugal spinner technique — ensuring uniform, void-free coverage throughout the entire pipe bore, including branches and flanged connections. The external surfaces received a high-build protective coating system for environmental protection.

1

Surface Preparation — Internal & External

Full abrasive blast cleaning to SA 2.5 / NACE 2 on both internal and external surfaces. All mill scale, rust, and contaminants removed. Surface profile verified with replica tape to ensure proper mechanical key for coating adhesion.

2

Internal Glass Flake Vinyl Ester Lining

Multi-coat glass flake vinyl ester lining applied to the internal bore using a centrifugal spinning tool. The spinner rotates at high speed inside the pipeline, distributing the coating evenly across the entire circumference. This method achieves consistent DFT (dry film thickness) and eliminates holidays — critical for chlorine service where even a pinhole can initiate rapid corrosion.

3

External Protective Coating

High-build exterior coating system applied to protect against the power plant environment — chemical splash, humidity, UV exposure, and mechanical damage during handling and installation. Finished in a high-visibility color for easy identification of chlorine service lines.

4

Inspection & Quality Assurance

Comprehensive QA program: holiday (spark) testing at full test voltage on all internal surfaces, DFT verification at multiple points, adhesion pull-off tests, visual inspection, and full documentation package delivered to the client for their maintenance records.

Completed — Internal & External Coating
03 The Results

Total Protection, Inside and Out

The dual coating system provided the client with a fully protected chlorine pipeline — internally lined with glass flake vinyl ester for chemical resistance and externally coated for environmental durability. The centrifugal spinner application method ensured 100% coverage with consistent film thickness, eliminating the risk of holidays and weak spots that traditional brush or spray methods can leave behind.
