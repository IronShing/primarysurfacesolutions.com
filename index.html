Who We Are

Built on Decades of
Oil & Gas Excellence

Primary Surface Solutions is a Kuwait-based industrial coatings company founded by a team of seasoned professionals with over 30 years of combined experience in the oil and gas sector. We understand the extreme conditions that industrial assets face — from blistering Gulf heat to corrosive marine environments.

As a proud sister company of Primary Auto TRD, we bring the same commitment to quality and customer satisfaction that has made our automotive division a trusted name in Kuwait.

Our core expertise lies in applying Rhino Linings polyurea coatings over spray polyurethane foam (SPF) for superior roof protection, and we are actively expanding into glass flake vinyl ester lining systems for the most demanding chemical and marine applications.

What We Do

Industrial Coating Solutions

From roof systems to pipeline protection, we deliver coatings engineered for extreme performance in the harshest environments.

Polyurea Roof Coatings

Our flagship service — Rhino Linings polyurea applied over spray polyurethane foam (SPF) creates a seamless, waterproof barrier that withstands Kuwait's extreme heat and UV exposure. Ideal for commercial and industrial rooftops.

  • Seamless, monolithic membrane
  • Superior UV and thermal resistance
  • 20+ year service life
  • Energy-efficient insulation

Industrial Protective Coatings

Heavy-duty polyurea and polyurethane systems for tanks, secondary containment, water treatment facilities, and structural steel. Engineered for chemical resistance, impact protection, and rapid return to service.

  • Tanks & secondary containment
  • Chemical & abrasion resistance
  • Rapid cure — return to service in hours
  • ASTM-tested performance
New Capability

Glass Flake Vinyl Ester

Our newest offering for the most demanding environments — glass flake vinyl ester linings provide unmatched chemical resistance and permeation barrier properties for marine, petrochemical, and desalination applications.

  • Superior chemical resistance
  • Excellent permeation barrier
  • Marine & offshore applications
  • Petrochemical & desalination

Pipeline Coatings

Protect critical oil & gas infrastructure with advanced polyurea and fusion-bonded epoxy pipeline coatings. Our team's three decades of field experience means we understand pipeline environments like no one else.

  • Internal & external pipeline lining
  • Corrosion prevention systems
  • Field joint coating
  • Rehabilitation of existing lines
